Diagnosing the Stated-Belief-to-Action Gap in LLM Sequential Decision Agents

Vincent Marciano Fan, HKUST
Workshop on Who Verifies the Agents? Toward Reliable Agent Development, NeurIPS 2026
LinkedIn · GitHub

A small repeated-pricing game with exact oracles, built so an LLM agent's stated belief and its action can be scored separately: is the belief close to the correct one, and does the action follow from the agent's own stated belief?

  • At the paper's fixed settings (no hidden reasoning, temperature 0), the frontier model's stated beliefs were mostly right, but it played the same quote on 99% of decisions.
  • The weaker model failed on both the belief and the action.
  • A cheap scaffold that carries the belief forward widened the frontier model's belief error compared with a token-matched control.

Limitation: the frontier model's actions barely vary at these settings, so this instrument cannot detect an action effect there.
